Outlier: three pixel-art directions

Every frame is a 320×180 canvas drawn one pixel at a time and scaled up by a whole number. Fixed palette, hard outlines, dithered shading, no anti-aliasing, and a bitmap font for the UI, so the world and the interface share the same pixel grid. Left: the question screen. Right: mid-flight after a rare answer.

AOffice chair launch

The current idea, redrawn as real pixel art. A rare answer sends your chair through the window, over the city, across the sea and up to the Moon.

BRocket launch

Every answer is fuel. Common answers barely clear the tower; rare ones fly past the Moon, Mars, the asteroid belt and Saturn. Distance in kilometres.

CDeep dive

A submarine dives instead of flying. The rarer the answer, the deeper you go: reef, shipwreck, twilight zone, anglerfish, the trench. Weird answers find weird creatures.
